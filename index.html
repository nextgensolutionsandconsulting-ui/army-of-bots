Army of Bots Risk Service

Army of Bots is a private, single-operator software project for simulated trading research and risk monitoring. The Risk Service integration is under development and is intended to retrieve account balances, equity and positions from the operator’s Alpaca PAPER account.

Integration scope

The proposed Risk Service requests read-only account access. It is not intended to submit, modify or cancel orders. The wider Army of Bots project explores automated decision-making and simulated execution; operational trading functionality is not released.

Availability

No public sign-up, commercial distribution, investment advisory service or real-money trading is offered. Alpaca integration is subject to applicable approval and technical validation. This informational website does not connect to brokerage accounts or collect credentials.
